01

The Challenge & Business Context

Industrial manufacturing and boiler operations face escalating fossil fuel prices and increasingly stringent carbon emission regulations across India. While biomass pellets provide a sustainable alternative, plant managers often hesitate due to inconsistent calorific value (GCV), moisture variations, and uncertain return on investment.

Furthermore, procurement decision-makers span corporate executives and regional ground plant supervisors who require technical specifications, RFQ inquiries, and savings projections in both English and Hindi with zero friction.

02

Engineering Architecture & Solution

Real-Time Financial ROI & Carbon Offsetting Model

Built an intuitive financial simulation engine using Framer Motion and dynamic parametric sliders, enabling plant managers to instantly calculate monthly fuel cost cuts and annualized CO₂ tonnage offset when switching from coal, diesel, or furnace oil.

Engineered Technical Matrix

Developed an interactive specification comparison matrix highlighting Mahaurja's custom pellet engineering (target 5,000+ kcal/kg GCV, low ash, controlled moisture) compared against standard generic biomass fuels.

Zero-Latency Bilingual Engine (EN / HI)

Engineered an in-memory i18n translation system supporting fluid switching between Hindi and English without page refreshes, paired with a specialized RFQ funnel capturing boiler types, pellet diameters (6mm/8mm/10mm), and delivery schedules.

Cinematic Performance & Edge Asset Optimization

Built a modern industrial aesthetic using Next.js 16 and Tailwind CSS v4, utilizing responsive SVG flow diagrams and lazy-loaded WebP/MP4 media pipelines to achieve near-instant First Contentful Paint.
