01

The Challenge & Business Context

College campus canteens endure extreme lunch rush congestion where hundreds of students scramble to order, pay, and collect food in narrow 30-minute intervals. Students waste precious break time standing in physical queues, while canteen kitchen staff face chaotic handwritten slips, order mix-ups, stock-outs, and payment tracking disputes.

Third-party aggregator apps (Zomato/Swiggy) cannot serve this market due to high commission fees (20-30%), delivery fees unsuited for campus walking distances, and inability to handle instant token-based counter pickups.

02

Engineering Architecture & Solution

Progressive Web App (PWA) with Offline Resiliency

Built an installable, mobile-first PWA with instant loading, dynamic menu browsing, and persistent cart caching, allowing students to browse even in low-connectivity areas of campus.

Real-Time Kitchen Display System (KDS)

Engineered a live order-station screen for kitchen staff using Supabase Realtime websocket subscriptions, automatically moving orders through "Received → Preparing → Ready for Pickup" with auditory chime alerts.

Instant UPI & Unique Token Generation

Integrated frictionless Razorpay UPI intents. Once paid, the system instantly assigns a unique pickup token number displayed on both the student's phone and kitchen monitor, eliminating counterfeit order claims.

Staff Admin & Inventory Management

Developed instant item toggle (In Stock / Out of Stock), price overrides, sales telemetry, and daily revenue reporting for canteen concessionaires with zero technical onboarding required.

03

Quantified Results & Impact

Elimination of Lunch Queues: Students pre-order from lecture halls and pick up items directly when notified, eliminating 15-minute wait lines.

Sub-Second KDS Order Dispatch: Zero paper slips; orders sync across customer phones and kitchen terminals in real time.

High Vendor Adoption: Intuitive one-tap interface trained non-technical canteen kitchen staff in under 15 minutes.

100% Direct Channel: 0% middleman commission preserves thin margin economics for campus vendors.
