01

The Challenge & Business Context

Colleges traditionally rely on physical attendance registers where professors take 10-15 minutes of every lecture manually calling out names. Paper registers are vulnerable to proxy attendance, physical loss, calculation errors, and delays in aggregating monthly attendance percentages required for exam eligibility and university compliance.

Faculty members had to spend days at the end of each semester reconciling sheets, while students remained unaware of falling below the mandatory 75% attendance threshold until it was too late to remediate.

02

Engineering Architecture & Solution

Multi-Role Institutional Governance (RBAC)

Architected granular role-based permissions separating Super Admins (HOD / Deans), Department Academic Coordinators, Subject Professors, and Students with strictly enforced JWT authentication.

45-Second Rapid Lecture Roll Call

Designed a lightning-fast responsive roll-call matrix allowing faculty to mark 70+ students in under 45 seconds with batch toggle states, attendance lockouts, and proxy safeguards.

Automated Statutory Threshold Audits

Built automated background aggregators calculating real-time attendance thresholds (<75% defalcator alerts) with one-click official PDF exports formatted to university compliance standards.

Live Faculty Onboarding & Campus Adoption

Conducted direct faculty training sessions and smoothly migrated legacy records into PostgreSQL, achieving 100% daily adoption across IT and CSIT departments.

03

Quantified Results & Impact

Formally Commended by HOD: Received institutional commendation and certificate of excellence from Dr. Prashant Lakkadwala.

Saved 15+ Minutes Per Lecture: Reclaimed valuable instruction time by replacing manual name calling with instant digital roll entry.

100% Paperless Compliance: Completely eliminated physical register loss, proxy disputes, and end-of-semester manual reconciliation.

Transparent Student Thresholds: Students monitor their real-time attendance percentages, preventing surprise exam debarments.
